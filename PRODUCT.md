# Aperture Exams

## Register

product

## Users

Instructors prepare and review assessments at a desk in a bright classroom. Students take timed assessments on laptops and phones, often under pressure, and need clear progress and dependable recovery.

## Product Purpose

Deliver trustworthy multiple-choice assessments from authoring to published content, durable attempts, server scoring and understandable feedback. Make every consequential action and saved answer visible.

## Brand Personality

Composed, precise, encouraging. Use direct instructional language and factual results.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic CRUD scaffolds, decorative gradients, oversized metric tiles, distracting examination screens and ambiguous save states.

## Design Principles

- Protect concentration during exams.
- Expose progress and persistence clearly.
- Make publishing and submission deliberate.
- Present scores with context and accessible text.
- Keep instructor and student workflows distinct.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic landmarks, keyboard navigation, visible focus, labeled inputs, readable contrast, reduced motion and responsive layouts. Do not rely on color alone.
